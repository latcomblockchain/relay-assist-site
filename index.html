Services

What we do on a case

We work under your instructions and your coverage decisions. We do not sell insurance, and we do not provide medical treatment.

HOSP

Hospital visit and monitoring

A coordinator visits the patient, talks to the treating doctor and sends you a daily update until discharge.

MED

Medical reports, translated

Diagnoses, cost estimates and invoices from the hospital, sent to you in English or Russian.

GOP

Guarantee of payment liaison

We deliver your GOP to the hospital, confirm it was accepted and help avoid cash deposits from your client.

POL

Police report support

We go with the client to the police station, get a copy of the report and translate it.

BIKE

Bike recovery and rental disputes

Towing, photographed damage and a fair settlement with the rental shop.

FAM

Family and client updates

We keep the patient and family informed in their language, so your call centre gets fewer calls.
