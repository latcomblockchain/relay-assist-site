For travellers in Phuket

Had an accident in Phuket? We help with the hospital, insurer, police and rental shop.

In an emergency, call 1669 (ambulance) or 1155 (tourist police) first. Once you are safe, we take care of what comes next, in English, Russian or Thai.

Get help

What we can do for you

HOSP

Hospital and translation

We come to the hospital, talk to the doctor and explain your treatment and costs in your language.

INS

Your travel insurer

We contact your insurer, send the documents they need and push for a guarantee of payment, so you don't pay a large deposit.

DEP

Deposit and bills

We check the bill and help negotiate the deposit with the hospital.

POL

Police report

We go with you to the police station, get the report and translate it.

BIKE

Rental shop dispute

Damage photos, a fair settlement and your passport back.

FAM

Family updates

We keep your family informed, so you can rest.

Price

Fixed price, agreed firstWe tell you the price for your situation before we start. No surprises.
If you are insuredIf your insurer covers assistance, we work with them directly and you may pay nothing.
