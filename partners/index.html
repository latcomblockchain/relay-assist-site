For partners in Phuket

When your customer crashes, we take the case so your team doesn't lose the day.

Rental companies, hotels, villas, Muay Thai camps, dive centres and tour operators on Phuket all face the same thing: after an accident, someone has to go to the hospital, deal with the deposit and the insurer, the police report and the family. That someone can be us.

Talk to us

Ways to work together

RENT

Rental companies

Sell an "Accident support" add-on with your bikes and cars. If the customer crashes, we handle the hospital, insurer, police and family. Revenue share or a fixed fee per case.

STAY

Hotels and villas

One number for guest emergencies. We go to the hospital, deal with the deposit and the guest's insurer, and keep your front office out of it. Commission to you on every case.

CAMP

Camps and dive centres

Training injuries, diving incidents and scooter accidents. You refer the member or customer, we take it from there, in English, Russian or Thai.

DMC

Tour operators and DMCs

We take the complex insurance cases off your guides and duty staff, with one report per case for you and the insurer.

How a case works

1. You call or email usSend us the guest's name, where they are and their insurance, if any.
2. We go to the hospitalWe meet the patient, talk to the doctor, and contact the insurer for the guarantee of payment.
3. Police and rentalWe help with the police report and settle things with the rental shop.
4. You get a reportA short closing report on what happened and what was paid, for you and the insurer.
